01 · Executive summary

One platform. Every custom-jewelry enquiry, captured and qualified.

JewlX is a conversion and lead-generation platform built exclusively for the jewelry industry. It embeds white-labeled configurators, instant-quote forms and AI tools on a jeweler's existing website, so every visitor who wants a ring, a custom piece, grillz or a gold payout can configure it, see a price and leave their details.

What it replaces

  • "Contact us" forms that capture a name and nothing else
  • Instagram DMs and emails asking "how much would this cost?"
  • Manual quoting by the owner or the CAD designer
  • Losing bridal buyers to online giants that offer instant configuration

What it delivers

  • More leads: the website produces enquiries at volume, not a trickle

  • Less manual work: pricing, confirmations, CRM entry and CAD briefs are automated

02 · The problems jewelers face todayPart 1 · The opportunity
02 · The business problem

Your website is a brochure. Your customers expect a salesperson.

Jewelers spend on advertising, photography and a beautiful website, then hand the most valuable moment, the point where a visitor decides to ask for a price, to a generic contact form or a DM inbox. JewlX was built to fix the six failures that follow.

1. Enquiries with no information

"Hi, interested in a ring." No shape, no carat, no budget. Every lead needs a discovery call before anyone knows whether it is worth pursuing.

2. Slow, manual quoting

Each price request becomes a task for the owner or the CAD designer. Quotes take hours or days, and buyers move on to whoever answered first.

3. Losing bridal buyers to online giants

Blue Nile, Brilliant Earth and With Clarity let customers build a ring and see a price instantly. Local jewelers are benchmarked against that experience.

4. DM and inbox chaos

Instagram, WhatsApp, email and phone calls all carry price requests. Nothing is tracked, followed up consistently, or measurable.

5. No pipeline, no attribution

Leads never reach the CRM, or reach it by copy-paste. Paid-media teams cannot optimize against real conversions.

6. Unqualified consultations

Appointment slots are spent on visitors whose budget was never known. Sales staff cannot prioritize.

What jewelers tell us

"People message us for a price and we never hear back from them."
"We don't know a lead's budget until we've spent 30 minutes with them."
"Our website is a brochure, not a salesperson."
"Every quote is a manual job for me or my CAD guy."
"We get leads but no details, just a name and 'hi, interested in a ring'."
"We tried an AI tool and the renders looked fake."

The cost of doing nothing

A single custom engagement ring is typically worth several thousand dollars. Across the largest JewlX deployment, the average ring value requested through the widget is roughly $4,300. When a website converts price requests into vague enquiries, or into nothing at all, the loss is not a marketing statistic. It is unbuilt rings.

Three ways to convert. You choose the friction.

Not every jeweler wants to sell a $6,000 ring through a web checkout. Ring OS supports three pricing modes so the widget matches the way you actually sell.

Estimates + deposit

Most popular

No-deposit enquiry

Zero payment friction. The customer sees the estimate, leaves contact details, and the design is stored as a lead and synced to the CRM. Ideal for jewelers who close in-store.

Maximum lead volume

Full custom pricing

Dynamic pricing with every product pushed to Shopify and the setting plus diamond sold as two line items. Currently rolling out.

What arrives with every Ring OS order or lead

  • Style, shank, metal, shape, size and carat range
  • Stone type, carat, color, clarity, cut grade, polish, symmetry and fluorescence
  • Diamond certificate number, grading lab and certificate PDF link
  • Wholesale diamond price and markup price, for margin visibility
  • Estimated ring price, deposit amount and order type
  • Band width, personalized inscription and any other request
  • Currency and Shopify order number
  • A source marker identifying the transaction as a JewlX Ring Builder sale

Catalog built for you, controlled by you

During onboarding the jeweler selects preferred styles, shanks, shapes, metals and diamond types. JewlX generates a catalog from the master product database automatically. From there the jeweler enables or disables items, sets markups or fixed prices, chooses which carats and sizes to offer, and configures metal variants with karat and purity options, all from the dashboard.

A won design can be pushed straight into a CAD request with messaging, delivery and resize handling, and purchasable CAD add-ons: CAD Tweak, New CAD Request and CAD + Render. Manufacturing status is tracked per order.

Real-time order sync

Shopify order webhooks create matching orders in JewlX instantly, trigger order-update notifications, and push the purchase to the CRM as an order record so Add-to-Cart sales reach the pipeline too.

Who buys Ring OS

Engagement-ring retailers who already carry or source diamonds, run paid traffic or strong organic search, and are losing bridal buyers to online-native competitors. The typical trigger is a new website launch, a rebrand, or an owner tired of quoting every ring by hand.

How the estimate is calculated

10 · JewlX Agent

A jewelry-trained AI that sells while you sleep, and never quotes a price you did not set.

JewlX Agent is an AI sales assistant inside the Ring OS widget. It answers buyer questions about diamonds, metals and settings, recommends configurations and can apply them directly to the builder. It is trained on the jeweler's own catalog, pricing rules and FAQ, with a server-side pricing gatekeeper so it can never invent a number.

What the customer experiences

  • Natural conversation in the jeweler's tone, no emojis, brand-consistent
  • Guidance on the four Cs, lab versus natural, metal choice and budget trade-offs
  • Recommendations that update the builder live as the conversation progresses
  • Diamond suggestions read from the jeweler's actual inventory and attributes

What the jeweler controls

  • Twenty default FAQ questions with a progress tracker, plus unlimited custom question-and-answer pairs
  • Document upload: PDFs are parsed into a searchable knowledge base for the agent
  • Custom system prompt, model selection and temperature
  • Chat session analytics with intent classification per message

The pricing gatekeeper

Every price the agent mentions is computed server-side from the jeweler's pricing rules and live metal prices. The language model chooses what to say; the platform decides what it is allowed to quote. This is the difference between an AI you can put in front of a $5,000 purchase and one you cannot.

Also included: AI help for your team

Dashboard users get an in-app AI help assistant for platform questions, plus a ticketed Help Center with threaded replies from JewlX support.

Feature-gated and metered

JewlX Agent is a per-client feature toggle included in Ring OS and above. Conversations draw on the AI Wallet at a small fixed cost per message, and every plan includes a monthly chat allowance.
